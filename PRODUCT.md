## Platform

adaptive

## Stack

Flutter and Dart, sharing one codebase across web, iOS, and Android.

## Users

People who habitually snooze alarms, plus demo and portfolio audiences encountering the product as a comedic interactive experience. The primary use scene is a sleepy person holding a phone immediately after an alarm rings.

## Product Purpose

KYA™ — Know Your Alarm turns snoozing into an escalating compliance process. A user must complete deliberately excessive verification before receiving a comically short 30-second snooze. Success means the entire joke is understandable, playable, and repeatable from the first alarm through the alarm returning.

## Positioning

KYA is not a generic difficult alarm. Its unique mechanism is treating a snooze request like a regulated financial transaction: friendly at first, then increasingly bureaucratic, while remaining functional and intentionally designed.

## Operating Context

The core session begins on a ringing alarm, proceeds through a reason form, wakefulness CAPTCHA, AI Snooze Officer, fake processing, approval, a real 30-second countdown, and a returning alarm. It is designed for portrait phones but must remain usable in browsers and wider layouts.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- A user can play the complete flow without an account or backend.
- The first CAPTCHA rejection and AI decision may be deterministic so the demo timing and jokes remain reliable.
- Hostile interactions never permanently block progress or move controls outside safe bounds.
- A visible, reachable Stop Alarm escape remains available for safety.
- The app honors safe areas, system back behavior, reduced-motion preferences, keyboard access on web, and minimum touch targets.
- The initial release does not need background alarm scheduling, push notifications, authentication, a database, or a live language model.

## Product Principles

- Every screen betrays the user exactly once.
- Frustration is deliberate, bounded, and recoverable.
- Bureaucracy escalates visibly as the flow advances.
- Copy and timing carry as much of the comedy as illustration.
- The final 30-second approval is the strongest punchline.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, semantic labels, keyboard/focus support on web, 44–48 logical-pixel touch targets, reduced-motion behavior, scalable text, safe areas, and an emergency stop that is never subject to hostile mechanics.
