# KYA Core Flow

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A harmless bedside toy gradually becomes an institution. KYA refuses the generic alarm-dashboard layout; the product opens directly inside the ringing moment and makes every attempt to snooze tighten the bureaucracy.

**OWN-WORLD:** Alarm yellow, wake-up blue, awake red, compliance green, warm white, and ink. Friendly stages use large rounded forms and Fredoka-like display type; verification stages become denser, squarer, and more procedural. Alby is the recurring illustrated anchor, changing posture and authority with the interface.

**STORY:** The user expects a normal snooze, discovers a verification trap, completes redundant paperwork, fails a suspicious CAPTCHA, argues with an unreasonable officer, waits through dishonest processing, and receives a triumphant authorization for only 30 seconds.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A portrait alarm surface fills the device: oversized live time at upper center, a large happy Alby in the middle, friendly wake-up copy beneath, and a dominant red Snooze control within thumb reach. Stop Alarm is visibly secondary but always reachable. Wider web layouts frame the phone experience rather than stretching it into a dashboard.

**FORM:** An eight-state, forward-moving interactive narrative derived directly from the supplied visual storyboard. The signature interaction is the Snooze control dodging twice inside safe bounds before yielding; the broader motion grammar shifts from buoyant spring to rigid procedural progress while honoring reduced motion.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Scope

- Alarm, hostile snooze, KYA introduction, reason form, CAPTCHA, AI officer, processing, KYC identity wake check (live front-camera liveness scan), approval, countdown, and alarm return.
- Responsive Flutter delivery for web, iOS, and Android from one codebase.
- Deterministic local behavior for the first version; no backend or account system.
- Generated Alby raster assets are production assets. Interface icons remain Flutter-native and consistent.
